FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What services does Public Kitchen provide?

Public Kitchen provides a full suite of bulk meal distribution services across the UAE. We specialize in bulk labor camp meals, mosque meal distribution, Friday meals, Ramadan iftar and custom meal plans.

Does Public Kitchen deliver year-round or only during Ramadan?

We deliver year-round. While we are well-known for Ramadan Iftar boxes, we also provide ongoing bulk meals distribution services for labor camps, construction sites, mosques and community programs.

How can I order Ramadan Iftar Meal Boxes?

You can order Ramadan Iftar Meal Boxes via our website or WhatsApp. Charity Parcel Prices start from AED 6.99.

Which locations in the UAE does Public Kitchen serve?

Public Kitchen operates across the UAE for mosque distribution services. Bulk labor camp meals and event catering are also available throughout the UAE.

How do you ensure the meals are hygienic and high quality?

We prepare meals in a fully licensed kitchen using fresh ingredients. Packaging and delivery processes maintain high hygiene standards.

Can I customize my meal plan or menu?

Yes. Public Kitchen offers customizable meal plans including high-protein meals, vegetarian options, specific portion sizes and other dietary requirements.

What is the process for mosque or Friday meal distribution?

The process includes consultation, menu selection, packaging and delivery planning, execution with the delivery team and feedback for future distributions.

Why should I choose Public Kitchen over other bulk meal providers?

Public Kitchen combines affordability, quality and reliability with flexible menu options and experience in large-scale food logistics.

What is the minimum lead time for ordering charity meal boxes?

For best results in bulk ordering, especially during Ramadan, it is recommended to place orders with sufficient lead time so production and delivery can be scheduled.

How do I contact Public Kitchen for bulk orders?

You can reach Public Kitchen via WhatsApp or phone at +971 52 686 0022, or email info@publickitchen.ae.
